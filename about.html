About NHA EduTech

We believe good software starts with understanding the people who will use it.

Practical ideas. Thoughtful development.

NHA EduTech is an independent software initiative focused on solving everyday problems through well-designed applications.

Our work began with challenges in education, where even a simple event can involve complicated planning, records and communication. That experience continues to shape how we approach software: keep it useful, clear and manageable.

Current areas of exploration include business operations, accessible learning tools and mobile-first services.

What matters to us

  • Understanding the problem before building
  • Clear communication and realistic project scope
  • Simple, accessible interfaces
  • Security and responsible handling of information
  • Software that can be maintained and improved
