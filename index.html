Our work

Applications for schools and websites for businesses.

Career Spotlight Day

A booking and timetabling platform for an annual careers event at a large secondary school in London. Students sign up with their school username and rank their career choices, and the system allocates every student to sessions automatically.

  • Student data imported from the school's Arbor MIS
  • Preference-based allocation for 1,350+ students
  • Sign-up tracking by year group and form, plus a form tutor portal

Sports Day Scoring System

Volunteers enter results from any phone in three taps, and the house leaderboard updates for students and staff as the day goes on. The PE department manages athletes, year rollover and the archive from one dashboard.

  • House leaderboard that updates as results are entered
  • PIN-protected result entry for volunteers
  • Automatic double points for relays
