Software as a service

For platforms such as ride-hailing, you do not have to buy and run the software yourself. We build it, then license it to you as a service.

The licence covers access to the platform, routine maintenance and security updates, bug fixes and technical support. New features, third-party costs such as maps, SMS and payment gateways, and hosting are agreed separately. We retain ownership of the underlying code, and you receive a licence to use the platform under agreed terms.

Two ways to work with us

Bespoke build. A system designed and built for you, with support afterwards.

Software as a service. A platform delivered under a one-off setup fee and a monthly licence, scoped in phases so you can launch early and grow.

How a project runs

You see progress at every stage and nothing is built without your approval.

  1. DiscoveryWe talk through your organisation, your current challenges and what the system needs to do.
  2. ProposalA clear written proposal covering scope, timeline and price.
  3. DesignWireframes or mockups, so you see how it will look and work before any code is written.
  4. BuildWe build in focused stages and share progress as we go.
  5. TestingWe test thoroughly, then invite you to try it and give feedback.
  6. LaunchWe deploy to your web address, set up your domain and check everything works.
  7. SupportBug fixes, small changes and ongoing support after launch.

Security and confidentiality

Every system we build runs over HTTPS, uses role-based access control, and stores passwords securely rather than in plain text. Where it is needed, we add audit logging that records who accessed what and when.

We do not share client data with third parties, and we are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before any detailed discussion of your requirements.
